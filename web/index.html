⚡ PPI — Pocket Pressure Index original metric

PFF only tells you whether a QB was hit, hurried or sacked — a yes/no flag. PPI measures how much heat the quarterback actually felt, as a continuous 0–100 score built entirely from the frame-by-frame tracking between the snap and the throw: rusher proximity (weighted exponentially), closing speed (velocity aimed at the QB), and pocket urgency (time-to-throw).
